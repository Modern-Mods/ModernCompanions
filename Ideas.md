# Ideas


## Scratch

- I want to add a new option to the mod; Companions show chat bubbles show over their heard when speaking, instead of chat.
    - This should be a toggle between chat speak and bubble speak, or both so the player can choose.
- Companions will interact with eachother
    - Chat amongst eachother
    - Call out enemies to eachother
    - Mourn companion deaths
    - Trash talk enemies
    - Ask other companions for food
        - Companions will be able to share edible food between themselves
- Firearm Support
    - Add support for popular firearm mods so Companions can use firearms
- Magic Support
    - Add support for popular magic mods so players can have magic wielding companions (mages, sorcerers, wizards, witches, warlocks, necromancers, etc.)



- Sophisticated Backpack Support
    - Companions will be able to equip a backpack, and the player can access the companion's backpack via GUI button on the companion inventory
    - Backpack will display visually on their backs as if a player equipped it
    - 'Backpack' button will go above the 'Curios' button
    - Button only present when Sophisticated Backpacks is present
- Ability to turn off all hostile actions towards villagers
    - When turned off, all damage to villagers should be disabled - even 'accidental' damage like arrow crossfire, or explosion/fire.
    - When turned off, Companions will NOT attack Villagers if the player attacks them.
- Companions should never target other players, unless explicity told to 
    - Unless told they can harm players, they should not even be able to damage players 'by mistake' - 'accidental' damage like arrow crossfire, or explosion/fire.
    - Think of it as a PvE/PvP toggle
    - Toggle should be to the left of the 'Release' button and should display 'Friendly' and green when in PvE, and 'Hostile' and Red when in PVP mode. 
    - When set to 'Friendly' the Companion will NEVER attack any OTHER player that their owner attacks.
- Each Companion will have a favorite food, which will increase their happiness and loyalty faster than other foods. 
    - This will be a random food per companion
    - Favorite food will be displayed in Bio
